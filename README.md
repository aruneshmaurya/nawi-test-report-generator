# TARAZOO (तराज़ू) — National Legal Metrology & NAWI Verification Platform

**TARAZOO** is an official Government-grade web portal built for the Department of Consumer Affairs, Legal Metrology Division (Government of India). It automates OIML R-76-1:2006 test report generation, calibration verification, and Section 24 digital certification for Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments (NAWIs).

### Key Features
- **Official GovTech Branding**: Designed for government presentation with national trust badges, bilingual headings, and OIML R-76 compliance standards.
- **Fast Evaluation & Demo Login**: Interactive 1-click auto-login and credential quick-filling on the login portal for evaluation officers and administrators.
- **Automated Metrological Calculation**: Real-time error (E), corrected error (Ec), Maximum Permissible Error (MPE) evaluation, repeatability variance, and eccentricity delta checks.
- **Verifiable Digital Certificates**: Tamper-proof QR-coded verification certificates compliant with the Legal Metrology Act, 2009.
- **Public Verification Registry**: Instant verification of instrument certification via `/verify/:reportNumber`.
